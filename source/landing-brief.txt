4 Design system
\002 Look: premium, calm and clinical. Use deep navy, muted teal, warm off-white and restrained accents. Avoid
sexualized imagery, flashing effects, stock photos of distressed couples, alarm graphics and \003miracle cure\004 visuals.
\002 Type: generous headline size, comfortable line spacing, short paragraphs and high contrast. One consistent
button style.
\002 Imagery: a genuine professional photograph of Dr. Basu, used with consent and accurate credentials. Subtle
abstract or architectural texture is preferable to patient-identifiable imagery.
\002 Layout: desktop hero in two columns \(copy and doctor photograph\); mobile in one column with headline and CTA
visible early. Keep line lengths short and generous whitespace.
5 Interactions worth building
\002 CTA: all primary buttons go to the same paid booking destination. Open a new tab only if the booking platform
requires it; preserve a clear return path.
\002 FAQ: accessible accordion with keyboard operation and visible focus state. Keep answers available in the HTML
for accessibility and search indexing.
\002 Mobile: optional sticky CTA after the hero scrolls away; it must not cover FAQ answers or payment information.
\002 Process: a three-step visual may show \003Book \006 Online discussion \006 Discuss next steps,\004 but only if that matches
the real booking flow. Do not imply a written plan, tests, prescription or follow-up is included unless confirmed.
\002 Accessibility: semantic headings, readable labels, sufficient color contrast, alt text for the clinician photograph,
tap targets and reduced-motion support. Avoid autoplay audio/video.
6 Conversion and measurement
Track page views, primary CTA clicks, booking start and completed paid bookings where the booking platform
permits. Keep analytics free of symptom details and other sensitive information. Review mobile behavior and
where visitors leave the booking flow before changing copy.
